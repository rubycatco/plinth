- **Sprite artwork** (`App/Resources/Skins`, `mallet.png`, `impact.png`, `censer.png`) was AI-generated for this project, with the edits and effects below. It is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Sprite effects and derived frames** are also CC0. The singing bowl's ripple is drawn with [`tools/draw_ripple.py`](tools/draw_ripple.py); the space key, bamboo block, moss stone, piston, CRT, abacus, cloud gong, bianzhong, stone chime, and temple fish pulses are drawn with [`tools/draw_skin_impacts.py`](tools/draw_skin_impacts.py). The pressed key, compressed piston, flicked abacus bead, swung cloud gong and bianzhong, and rocked stone chime frames are derived from their idle artwork, keeping their bases still. The seal's mallet head is its generated rosewood head recoloured to boxwood. The CRT's prompt and waveform are drawn over the same screen artwork, leaving its casing unchanged. The disk image's background (`App/DMGBackground.png`), with its ensō and seal, is drawn with [`tools/draw_dmg_background.py`](tools/draw_dmg_background.py). The app icon (`App/AppIcon.png`, and the iPhone app's in `iOS/Plinth/Assets.xcassets`) is the ink fish's idle artwork on the same rice paper, drawn with [`tools/draw_app_icon.py`](tools/draw_app_icon.py).
- **Knock sounds** are synthesized by the app as each knock is made (`Sources/PlinthCore/KnockSynth.swift`), not recorded. That synthesis is part of the MIT code.
- **Zen quotes** in `Sources/PlinthCore/Zen.swift` are quoted in the original Chinese from texts in the public domain: the Diamond Sutra, the Vimalakirti Sutra, the Platform Sutra, the Chan records (《景德传灯录》, 《碧岩录》, 《无门关》, 《五灯会元》), the Imperial Edition of Baizhang's Pure Rules, and poems by Wang Wei and Li Ao. The disk image's 「放下着」 is Zhaozhou's, from the same Chan records. Each line was checked against its source, the Buddhist texts against the [CBETA](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/) canon.
